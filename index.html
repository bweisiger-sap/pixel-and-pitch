Pixel & Pitch
2026 Recap

Pixel & Pitch is an experimental, cross-functional initiative hosted by DX that brings marketing employees together to think beyond their day-to-day roles and disciplines. Through collaboration and creative exploration, teams tackle SAP.com-related customer problems and design challenges, developing prototypes to pitch to a panel of judges and their peers.

Teams are encouraged to explore without the restrictions of current technologies, processes, or stakeholder dependencies that may typically be required, in order to see what's possible.

Each year an "Overall Winner" is selected by the judges, and an "Audience Favorite" is selected by all who attend the Pitch Day. The prototypes that are feasible are then built into current or future roadmaps where they are further developed and refined in partnership with the appropriate stakeholders and execution teams.

★ Overall Winner
Improving FAQs for a New World
AI Experiences
Team

Wei Cheng, Angela Zhen, Caerley McShane, Grace Townsend, Jenn League

Problem
SAP's FAQ components were built solely for human navigation, but AI crawlers and LLMs are now a second audience, and the current unstructured format means AI can't consistently interpret or reuse those answers, limiting SAP's visibility in AI-generated search results.
Solution
Transform FAQs from a static UI element into a governed, machine-readable knowledge layer with structured metadata, centralized answer repositories, and taxonomy aligned to buyer journey stage, creating one authoritative answer source for both human visitors and AI systems.
How it helps SAP
Increases SAP's visibility and authority in AI-generated search results, driving more qualified traffic before buyers even reach the site.

★ Audience Favorite
Experience Amplifiers
Micro-Moments of Delight
Team

Yewon Park, Keith Hart, Megan Hesse

Problem
SAP.com's focus on click-path efficiency has stripped any sense of delight from the user experience. Interactions are static and impersonal, making the site feel transactional rather than engaging.
Solution
A system of thoughtful micro-animations, including interactive hover effects, personalized video lightboxes, and an animated email capture form, that restore emotional engagement and make the site feel responsive and memorable without adding visual noise.
How it helps SAP
Strengthens brand perception and user confidence, making SAP.com feel premium and human, directly impacting engagement and form conversion rates.

A Living Conversation
AI Experiences
Team

Leanne McLaren, Ashley Aviles, Jaslyn Tan, Kalle Richert, Yeshwant Paul, Justin Lee

Problem
SAP.com asks visitors to navigate a product-first structure to find what's relevant to them, creating a one-way experience that doesn't meet buyers where they are.
Solution
Transform SAP.com into a Joule-powered living conversation where the site dynamically rebuilds itself around the visitor's stated challenge, surfacing relevant industry context, proof points, and solutions as a personalized dialogue.
How it helps SAP
Reduces friction in the buyer journey, increasing the likelihood of conversion by meeting customers at their problem instead of making them hunt for solutions.

On-Page Modal Product Experience for Cross-solution Areas
Invisible Value
Team

Natasha Paul Stephen, Natalia Jimenez, Christopher Chan, Ben Benitez, Praise Muparamoto

Problem
Visitors landing on industry pages are handed off to generic product pages with no context or relevance, causing warm leads to bounce before they engage.
Solution
An embedded, dynamic product tour that stays on-page and personalizes the experience through three qualifying questions, surfacing tailored product screenshots, customer stories, analyst quotes, and ROI data matched to that visitor's specific profile.
How it helps SAP
Turns passive page visitors into engaged prospects by making product relevance immediately obvious, reducing bounce and accelerating pipeline.

Gaining Trust in an AI World
Invisible Value
Team

Elaine Shih, Trevor Jenkins, Allison Lee, Jen Beatty, Kathryn Kamin, Laura Bejarano

Problem
Buyers are increasingly evaluating products through AI-generated summaries before visiting SAP.com, but SAP's content isn't structured for machine consumption, limiting its presence in that layer.
Solution
Pull third-party peer reviews (G2, TrustRadius) into SAP.com and Joule with structured, AI-readable markup and verified source links, allowing AI agents to cite SAP's social proof and enabling Joule to proactively address concerns rather than cherry-pick top ratings.
How it helps SAP
Builds buyer confidence through peer validation, shortening evaluation cycles and improving conversion by letting real customer evidence do the selling.
